How we work

No estimates. No assumptions presented as fact. No gaps in the record filled with what is convenient.

Every figure is traced to source and confirmed before publication. Every gap in the available record is disclosed, not filled. Every conflict between sources is stated, not resolved in favor of the account that reads more cleanly.

This distinction matters most where capital is at risk. A due diligence report that omits an inconvenient contingency does not fail quietly. It fails when the exposure is realized, and by then the cost has moved from the page to the balance sheet.

The standard applied here is the one institutional risk committees already hold themselves to internally. It does not soften because the client is smaller, the timeline is shorter, or the finding is inconvenient.
