<div class="page-heading">
  <h1>Datasets</h1>
</div>

<section class="section--tight">
  <p class="methodology-statement">A bank's audited and quarterly statements are public, but useful only in the format they were filed in: one institution, one period, one PDF at a time. Definitions and disclosure formats shift year to year and bank to bank. What's sold here isn't the filings themselves; it's the reconciled, comparable dataset built from them, filtered to the banks, years, and metrics actually needed.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Scope</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">Covers all Kenyan banks, not restricted to a tier or subset. Audited annual and quarterly financial statements form the core. Expanding to SACCOs and microfinance institutions is planned as a forward direction, not offered as live scope yet.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Who this is for</h2>
  <ul class="tier__list" style="max-width:60ch;">
    <li>Existing report clients: anyone who has commissioned a Counterparty Intelligence Report or Sector Intelligence Retainer already has the underlying structured data as an input to that engagement; the dataset is a natural low-friction addition</li>
    <li>Comparative and transaction buyers: anyone screening or comparing several banks at once who needs a clean side-by-side panel rather than reconciling several banks' disclosures by hand</li>
    <li>Researchers, journalists, and academics</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">What you get</h2>
  <ul class="tier__list" style="max-width:60ch;">
    <li>Audited and quarterly financial statement data, filtered to the specific banks, years, and metrics requested</li>
    <li>Derived metrics: ratios or trends not explicitly reported by the bank itself, calculated consistently across years and institutions</li>
    <li>Every figure traceable to its originating filing: source document, page, and date</li>
    <li>Restatements flagged: figures noted as originally filed versus as restated, where a bank has revised a prior-year number in a later filing</li>
    <li>Gaps marked, not filled: where a metric wasn't disclosed for a given year, that is stated rather than silently interpolated</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">What makes this different from pulling the filings yourself</h2>
  <ul class="tier__list" style="max-width:60ch;">
    <li>Reconciliation notes: a short note per bank flagging where a definition or reporting format changed year to year, and how it was reconciled</li>
    <li>A data dictionary: every metric precisely defined, including which source line items it draws from and any judgment calls made in deriving it</li>
    <li>A cover note per delivery: a short, factual note on anything notable in the specific cut requested, before the file is opened</li>
    <li>Dated and versioned: every delivery stamped as of a given date, compiled from filings through a given period</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Delivery</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">Delivered as structured files (CSV/Excel). Format matched to how the data will be used: long/tidy format for anyone feeding it into their own model, wide format for anyone reading it directly, confirmed at intake rather than assumed.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Sample</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">A small sample cut, two or three metrics for one bank, is available to first-time buyers so the actual sourcing, structure, and derived-metric quality can be seen before committing to a paid, filtered request.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">What this is not</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">Independently compiled from published disclosures, not verified, audited, or endorsed by the institutions themselves. Same sourcing discipline as every other service: the data is structured and traceable, not a certification of the underlying figures.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Licensing</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">Licensed for the buyer's internal use only: analysis, reporting, and internal decision-making. Redistribution, resale, or public republishing of the underlying dataset files is not permitted without a separate written licence.</p>
</section>
