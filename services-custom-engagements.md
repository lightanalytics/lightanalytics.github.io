<div class="page-heading">
  <h1>Custom Engagements</h1>
</div>

<section class="section--tight">
  <p class="methodology-statement">Not every question fits a standard deliverable. A specific transaction, a live situation triggered by a ruling or an acquisition announcement, a one-off investigation: these get the same evidence-based methodology as every other service, individually scoped to the question actually being asked.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Who this is for</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">For work that doesn't map cleanly onto Counterparty Intelligence Reports, SACCO &amp; MFB Due Diligence, the Sector Intelligence Retainer, or Datasets: a specific transaction question, a targeted investigation, or an advisory need scoped to a particular decision.</p>
  <p class="methodology-statement">Bounded, not open-ended. This is flexible application of the same expertise and sourcing discipline used everywhere else on this site, not an invitation for unrelated work outside that domain.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">What you get</h2>
  <ul class="tier__list" style="max-width:60ch;">
    <li>Individually scoped work, defined after an initial conversation</li>
    <li>Same sourcing and rigor standard as every other service: every claim traceable to a named, dated public source</li>
    <li>A written proposal following the scoping conversation, covering scope, deliverable, and timeline, not just a verbal quote</li>
    <li>A structured deliverable regardless of outcome: if the evidence doesn't support a finding, that absence is itself documented and delivered as the answer to the question asked, never an empty result</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Why a boutique, specifically</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">No multi-layered sign-off, no committee. Engagements move faster than a larger firm structurally allows, without a reduction in rigor. This matters most for live, time-sensitive situations: a ruling, a deal, a regulatory move, where the value is moving quickly on something current, not fitting into a scheduled review cycle.</p>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">How it works</h2>
  <ul class="tier__list" style="max-width:60ch;">
    <li>Initial conversation to understand the question and situation, confidential by default; a confidentiality agreement is available before any specifics are shared, if needed</li>
    <li>A written proposal covering scope, deliverable, and timeline</li>
    <li>A signed Terms of Reference before work begins, the same discipline applied to every engagement here, surfaced explicitly on this page since scope is least defined by default</li>
    <li>Delivery as a structured written report, same standard as the rest of the site</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">Response time</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">Initial replies within one to two business days. A person, not a queue, on the other end.</p>
</section>

<div class="policy-line">
  If a request matches one of the other services more closely, that is where it will be pointed instead. This page isn't a substitute for a defined product at a different price point.
</div>

<section class="section--tight">
  <h2 class="section-title">What this is not</h2>
  <p class="methodology-statement">Not legal advice. Not expert witness or litigation testimony. Not a KYC/AML screening service.</p>
